# Visual and Image Constraints

## Approved constraints

- The new website must not reproduce or closely imitate the layouts, component
  patterns, information hierarchy, or overall visual composition in the supplied
  Mobina screenshots.
- The screenshots may be used to recover provisional copy and identify source
  photographs of Dr. Ali Moradi.
- Blue remains the primary brand-color direction.
- The definitive blue palette will be sampled from the original supplied artwork
  and formalized as accessible design tokens during the visual-system milestone.
- Orange accents visible in the screenshots are not yet approved and must not be
  assumed to be part of the final palette.

## New design direction

The new system should be independently designed around Dr. Moradi's combined
identity as a surgeon, researcher, inventor, rehabilitation-robotics specialist,
and entrepreneur. It should avoid falling back to a generic clinic-template look.

Desktop is the primary design canvas, but every component and composition must be
defined for tablet and mobile rather than merely scaled down.

## Typography direction

- Use Manrope for English interfaces and content.
- Use Vazirmatn for Persian and Arabic interfaces and content.
- Treat typography, line length, rhythm, and density independently for LTR and RTL
  rather than forcing identical text geometry across languages.

## Doctor likeness requirement

When generating or editing any image containing Dr. Ali Moradi:

- Use an actual supplied photograph as the identity reference.
- Preserve his recognizable facial identity exactly; do not invent a similar person.
- Do not materially change facial geometry, age, skin tone, hairline, or distinctive
  features.
- Do not change clothing, pose, medical setting, or professional context in a way
  that could misrepresent a real credential, procedure, institution, or event.
- Generated composites must be internally labeled as generated or edited assets
  until the client approves them.
- Do not publish generated medical scenes without checking anatomical plausibility,
  equipment accuracy, and reputational risk.

## Source-image quality note

The current face references are embedded in composed screenshots rather than
provided as original portraits. They are sufficient to identify the subject, but
high-resolution original photographs are strongly preferred for accurate,
production-quality image generation and responsive crops.

## Asset policy

The four earlier design screenshots are intentionally not committed as approved UI
assets. If retained in the repository later, they must live in a clearly labeled
reference-only location and must not be shipped with the production website.
