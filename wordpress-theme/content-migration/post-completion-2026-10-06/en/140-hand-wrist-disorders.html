Understanding the symptom pattern

Pain, stiffness, clicking and numbness are different clues. Symptoms may arise from joints, tendons or nerves, and similar complaints can have different causes.

A focused assessment

Carpal tunnel syndrome involves pressure on the median nerve at the wrist. Pain after a fall may instead require assessment for a fracture. The history, examination and any appropriate tests help distinguish these conditions.

Preparing for a consultation

Note when symptoms began, which fingers are affected, whether they wake you at night and which tasks are difficult. Bring previous imaging and a list of treatments tried. Discuss both symptom relief and the everyday activities you want to regain.
