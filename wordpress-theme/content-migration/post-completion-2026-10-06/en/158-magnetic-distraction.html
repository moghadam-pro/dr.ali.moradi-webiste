A research-led approach to controlled joint distraction and tissue preservation.

The research question

Magnetic joint distraction explores whether magnetic repulsion can create a sustained separating force at a joint. The archived project focuses on the thumb carpometacarpal joint.

The proposed design

The project describes two magnet-containing implants in protective capsules, positioned so like poles oppose one another. The illustrations explain the design concept, not a standard treatment protocol.

Context before conclusions

Design, fixation, tissue interaction and clinical evaluation are distinct stages of device development. A prototype image or certificate does not, by itself, establish safety or effectiveness for a patient. The dedicated innovation page separates the concept, project figures and archived documents so they can be reviewed in context.
