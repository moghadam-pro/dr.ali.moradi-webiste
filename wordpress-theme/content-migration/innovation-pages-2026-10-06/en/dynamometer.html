Innovation project

Dynamometer

Monitoring distraction force during dynamic distal-radius external fixation.

1

Clinical problem

During closed treatment of distal-radius fractures, excessive traction may increase tension on the tendons—particularly the extensor tendons—and contribute to stiffness or reduced finger motion.

2

Concept

The dynamometer is used with the dynamic distal-radius external fixator so the treating team can check the applied distraction force during surgery and at later follow-up visits.

3

Two reading systems

The archived design describes a quantitative scale for direct force measurement and a colour scale with green, yellow, and red zones to indicate increasing force levels.
