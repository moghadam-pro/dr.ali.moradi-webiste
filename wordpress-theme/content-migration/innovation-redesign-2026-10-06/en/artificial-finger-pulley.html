Innovation project

Artificial finger pulley

A reconstructive-device concept for replacing or supporting the finger pulley mechanism during tendon reconstruction.

Project overviewProject galleryReference sources

Project overview

01

Role of the pulley system

Flexor pulleys guide the tendons and convert linear muscle-tendon movement into rotation and torque at the finger joints. Lacerations of the hand may damage this system and require reconstruction.

02

Reconstruction challenge

Conventional reconstruction may use pulley remnants, bone tunnels or material encircling the phalanx. Early active movement can stretch a newly reconstructed annular pulley, which is one reason staged tendon-grafting procedures are considered.

03

Artificial pulley concept

The proposed device combines a titanium chassis with a tubular body. It is designed to support tendon gliding and investigate a single-stage alternative to a two-stage grafting procedure. This is a development objective rather than a claim of established superiority.
