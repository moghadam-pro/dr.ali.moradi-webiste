Working at a small scale

Microsurgery uses magnification and fine instruments to work on small structures, including blood vessels and nerves. It is a technique used within a wider reconstructive plan.

Reconstruction as a team effort

Complex hand injuries may require decisions about tissue coverage and function, as well as the injured structures. The choice of procedure depends on the injury and the condition of the tissues.

Questions worth asking

Ask what the proposed reconstruction aims to achieve, what alternatives exist and how follow-up will be organised. Recovery also involves wound care, protection and rehabilitation. Your team can explain the plan for your individual injury and what changes should be reported promptly.
