The supplied pair documents an active surgical reconstruction and contains graphic medical content, visible blood, and potentially identifiable patient anatomy.

Rules:

- Do not use these images on the homepage, navigation, social preview, or unexpected autoplay media.
- Publish only after written patient-consent and rights confirmation.
- Present them behind a clear sensitive-content warning on a relevant clinical case page.
- Do not use AI reconstruction, beautification, or generative alteration on clinical evidence images.
- Allowed processing is limited to orientation, color consistency, exposure correction, privacy masking where required, and non-misleading crops.
