Project overview

01

Schanz pins and fixation pins

The source presents Schanz pins as pointed, threaded metal rods used to connect bone to an external fixation frame. Different lengths and diameters serve different fixation requirements.

02

Design variants

The catalogue includes self-tapping, self-drilling and tapered-thread designs, alongside simple and coated Schanz families. Fixation pins may be used for small fragments that cannot accept screws, including selected hand and foot fractures.

03

Product documentation

The reference provides catalogue access for pins and external fixators. The current page does not specify a coating composition or provide comparative coating results; those details should be checked in the applicable product documentation.
