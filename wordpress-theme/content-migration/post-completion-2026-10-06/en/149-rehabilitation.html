Individual follow-up and early rehabilitation guidance after treatment.

Rehabilitation has a purpose

Hand therapy supports a return to useful movement and daily activities after injury or surgery. The plan should reflect the structures affected and the stage of healing.

A plan that changes over time

A therapist may use education, a suitable orthosis and guided exercises. The allowed movement and any later strengthening are adjusted to the treatment plan; more force or more repetitions are not automatically better.

Make progress practical

Bring the surgeon’s instructions to therapy and explain which tasks matter at home or work. Ask for a clear home programme and review it at follow-up. Record difficulties so the team can adjust the plan rather than replacing exercises with an unrelated online routine.
