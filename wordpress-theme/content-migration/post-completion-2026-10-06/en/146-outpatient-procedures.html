Planning an outpatient visit

Some hand procedures can be arranged without an overnight hospital stay. Suitability depends on the planned procedure, anaesthesia and the patient’s health and support at home.

Before the procedure

Bring your medication list, relevant imaging and previous reports. Discuss allergies and any medical conditions with the team. Follow the preparation instructions supplied for your own procedure and ask whether you need an accompanying person.

Before going home

Make sure you understand the dressing instructions, permitted hand use, prescribed medicines and follow-up appointment. Keep the clinic’s contact information available. Do not assume that another patient’s fasting or recovery instructions apply to you; check any uncertainty with the treating team.
