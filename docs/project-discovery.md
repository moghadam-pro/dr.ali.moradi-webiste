# Project Discovery

## Executive summary

This website is not intended to be a conventional clinic website. Its primary
purpose is to establish a unified scientific and professional personal brand for
Dr. Ali Moradi and communicate the breadth of his work at first glance.

The experience should connect five dimensions of his professional identity:

1. Hand surgeon
2. Researcher
3. Innovator and inventor
4. Rehabilitation-robotics specialist
5. Entrepreneur

## Professional ecosystem

### Hand surgery

- Clinical expertise and services
- Professional background and credentials
- Hand and orthopaedic treatment areas

### Research

- Scientific articles
- Research projects
- Academic collaborations
- Research-center activities

### Innovation and inventions

Known examples mentioned during discovery include:

- A magnetic-joint concept intended to reduce articular friction in arthritis
- Medical equipment for particular types of fractures
- Artificial-hand solutions

All technical and medical claims require client-supplied, approved wording before
publication.

### Rehabilitation robotics

- Artificial hands
- Prostheses
- Three-link and five-link variants
- Rehabilitation robots

### Entrepreneurship

Dr. Moradi owns or leads Avista, a medical-equipment company involved in product
development and the commercialization of innovations. Phase 1 will introduce the
company and link to its existing website rather than reproduce its catalogue.

## Experience direction

The home page must immediately communicate that Dr. Moradi is more than a surgeon.
The previously explored themes below received positive feedback and are retained as
an initial creative direction, subject to final copy approval:

- Innovation
- Application
- Back to Life

## Key audiences

- Patients and their families
- Researchers and academic collaborators
- Medical professionals and students
- Investors and industry partners
- Visitors interested in inventions and rehabilitation robotics

## Known risks

- **Unconfirmed visual preference:** collect websites and visual references liked by
  Dr. Moradi before committing to a UI direction.
- **Broad professional scope:** use clear information architecture so each audience
  can find a relevant path without fragmenting the personal brand.
- **Insufficient photography:** identify required shots early and arrange a
  professional photo session where necessary.
- **Legacy web properties:** treat older websites as content references, not visual
  constraints.
