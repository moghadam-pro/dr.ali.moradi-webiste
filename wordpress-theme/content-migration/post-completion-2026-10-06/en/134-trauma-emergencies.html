Fractures, complex wounds, and tendon, nerve, or vascular injuries.

Assessing an injured hand

A fall, cut or crush injury can affect more than the skin. Examination checks finger movement, sensation, blood supply and bone alignment; X-rays may be needed to identify a fracture.

When care is urgent

A deep wound, visible deformity, uncontrolled bleeding or a pale, cold finger needs urgent assessment. A small skin wound does not reliably show the extent of the injury beneath it.

Planning recovery

Treatment is selected for the injured structures and the patient’s needs. Ask which movements are permitted, how to protect the hand and when follow-up is due. Keep any imaging and discharge instructions for the next visit.
