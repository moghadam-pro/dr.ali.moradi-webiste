How an external frame works

An external fixator uses pins or screws placed in bone and connected to a frame outside the limb. The frame supports alignment while the treatment team manages the fracture and surrounding tissues.

Choosing a fixation strategy

The role of the frame depends on the fracture and soft-tissue condition. It may form part of a staged treatment or remain during healing. Different designs address different anatomical and mechanical requirements.

Research and daily care

The innovation catalogue presents dedicated projects on external fixation. Device development and patient treatment are separate questions: a project description does not establish suitability for a particular fracture. Patients should obtain their own pin-site, movement and follow-up instructions from the treating team.
