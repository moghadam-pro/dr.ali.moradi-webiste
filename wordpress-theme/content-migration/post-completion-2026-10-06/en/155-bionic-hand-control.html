Magnetic sensing and control research for more intuitive prosthetic-hand function.

From a signal to a movement

A bionic hand needs both a mechanical structure and a control system. The control system interprets an input and translates it into a command for the hand’s motors.

Related research projects

The innovation catalogue separates the H3 and H5 hand projects, training software and magnetic control of an artificial limb. These are related development topics rather than interchangeable products.

Reading the project information

Review each project’s description and illustrations to understand its intended role. A training interface, a sensor concept and a mechanical hand answer different questions. An engineering illustration is not a clinical result or a promise of a particular level of function. Use the linked project pages for the original project context.
