Carpal tunnel, tendon disorders, tendon injuries, and reconstruction.

Different structures, different symptoms

Nerves carry signals for sensation and movement. Tendons connect muscles to bones. Numbness, weakness or difficulty bending a finger after an injury can therefore reflect different problems.

The role of examination

The surgeon checks sensation and each finger’s movement, alongside the wound and circulation. A cut may involve both a tendon and a nerve; the treatment plan depends on what is damaged.

Protecting a repair

After repair, protection and carefully planned rehabilitation work together. Follow the specific splint and movement instructions given to you. Ask the team before starting strengthening exercises or changing a splint; another patient’s exercise plan may not suit your repair.
