Innovation project

Magnetic control for an artificial limb

A research programme investigating magnetic sensing and control interfaces for more intuitive activation of an artificial upper limb.

1

Muscle movement as a control signal

This research investigates magnetic sensing of residual forearm muscle movement to control a bionic limb. Implanted magnetic tags allow external sensors to detect the changes associated with contraction.

2

Surgery and intention decoding

The source describes a flexor–extensor transfer and magnet implantation approach. An artificial neural network extracts intended movement directly from magnetic-field signals (KMG), rather than relying only on tracking the location of a tag.

3

Clinical research record

The project page documents an initial upper-limb clinical implementation with surgical-stage videos, a post-operative demonstration and an animation. Research and patent resources accompany the description. These are records of the project at the time of publication.
