Innovation project

Bionic Hand H5

A multi-grip prosthetic hand with independently powered fingers and configurable control.

1

Design

The archived H5 description presents five independently powered fingers, a manually adjustable thumb, five motors, and wrist movement for a total of seven degrees of freedom.

2

Control

The system is described as supporting EMG- or KMG-based control, independent finger movement, proportional response, multiple grip modes, and force feedback.

3

Mechanical safeguards

The product concept includes non-back-drivable motion, collision-force absorption, slip detection, and a safety-release mechanism for daily tasks.
