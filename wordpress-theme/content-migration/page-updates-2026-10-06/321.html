After-surgery instructions.

Instructions for patients following hand and upper-extremity surgery in Dr. Ali Moradi’s service.

01

Dressing care

After surgery, do not open the dressing or allow it to get wet.

02

Follow-up appointment

Your next visit is one week after surgery, unless a different date is specified at your in-person visit on the first day after surgery.

03

Arm positioning and movement

Keep your arm supported in a sling suspended from your neck. Open and close your fingers only if your surgeon has specifically permitted this movement.

04

Prescribed medicines

Collect your prescribed medicines from the ward nursing station after discharge. If an antibiotic is not prescribed, do not take one on your own.

05

Problems or intolerable pain

Contact the care team via Telegram or SMS at +98 905 450 1141 if you experience a problem or intolerable pain.
