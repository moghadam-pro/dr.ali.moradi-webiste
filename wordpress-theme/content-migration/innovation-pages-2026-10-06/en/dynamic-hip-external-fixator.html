Dynamic hip external fixator

An external-fixation product pathway for dynamic management around the hip, documented by the Avisa medical-device catalogue.

1

Clinical context

Intertrochanteric hip fractures often affect older people with additional medical conditions. The source describes an external fixation approach intended to reduce the operative burden while permitting stabilization of the fracture.

2

Dynamic design

The fixator has a sliding head that allows compression across the fracture. Its pin arrangement is positioned away from the perineal hygiene area, and the frame is designed for stable three-dimensional fixation of the femur.

3

Application and documentation

The product description presents insertion under sedation and local anaesthesia as part of its proposed approach. The reference includes research, patent, catalogue and demonstration resources. Selection and operative technique depend on the treating surgical team.
