Innovation project

Lag plate

A plate-development concept intended to combine lag-screw mechanics with a dedicated fixation construct.

1

Compression principle

A lag screw compresses fracture fragments by engaging the far cortex while gliding through the near cortex. Tightening the head draws the fragments together; its relationship to the fracture plane determines the direction of compression.

2

Plate construction

The Lag plate combines a hook engaging one fragment with a conventional AO dynamic-compression hole on the other side. Fixing the plate to one fragment while allowing controlled sliding at the other transfers compression through the construct.
