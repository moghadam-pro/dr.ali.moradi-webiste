Innovation project

Bionic-hand training software

A software pathway for learning, configuring, and practising control of the Integrom bionic-hand system.

Project overviewProject galleryReference sources

Project overview

01

Clinical setup and feedback

IntegLearn supports prosthetic-control training using real-time EMG visualization. Clinicians can examine electrode placement and signal quality, use manual or smart calibration, and configure an individualized rehabilitation pathway with progress reports.

02

Three control modes

Position control trains sustained contraction and precise positioning. Velocity control uses contractions to regulate movement speed. Force control uses a hand visualizer to practise modulation of grip force.

03

Guided exercises

Paddle, Flappy Bird and Target Bar exercises practise accuracy, sustained activation and rapid adjustment. A 3D Hand Visualizer connects the exercises to grasp control. Patients follow clinician-approved activities while the Rehab Path Scheduler organizes training and tracks progress.
