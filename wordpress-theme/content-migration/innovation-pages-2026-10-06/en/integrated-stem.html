Innovation project

Integrated stem

An intramedullary concept for trans-radial osseointegration challenges.

1

Clinical-engineering challenge

Forearm osseointegration is constrained by the small diameter and variable curvature of the radius and ulna, together with two-bone mechanics and rotational movement.

2

Design response

The archived project describes a segmented intramedullary radius stem intended to adapt to this anatomy while supporting an integrated prosthetic interface.

3

Development status

The page presents a design direction rather than general clinical guidance; safety, fixation, loading, and long-term outcomes require formal evaluation.
