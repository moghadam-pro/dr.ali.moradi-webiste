Hip Exoskeleton HEXA

A wearable robotic system developed to assist gait rehabilitation.

1

Purpose

The archived project describes HEXA for people with lower-limb disability, including gait impairment after hemiplegic stroke and scissoring gait.

2

Actuation

Hip-joint actuators use geared 70-watt brushless motors connected to thigh links, with the aim of supplying hip-flexion torque during the gait cycle.

3

Rehabilitation goals

The listed goals include addressing gait abnormalities and supporting symmetry, stride, linear speed, and walking duration under supervised rehabilitation.
