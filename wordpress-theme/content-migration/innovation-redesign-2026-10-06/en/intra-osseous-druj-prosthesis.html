Intra-osseous DRUJ prosthesis

A high-technology development concept for an intra-osseous prosthetic solution at the distal radioulnar joint.

Project overviewProject galleryReference sources

Project overview

01

Intra-osseous construction

A segment of the ulna is replaced by an implant with two stems seated in the remaining proximal and distal bone. The design aims to preserve the triangular fibrocartilage complex (TFCC) and places the pivot close to the natural distal radioulnar joint.

02

Joint mechanics

The implant is intended to transmit forearm rotation through its pivot while maintaining stability. The source discusses use where the TFCC is intact or only mildly impaired, and conversion of a Sauvé–Kapandji construct.

03

Evaluation

The manufacturer reports biomechanical evaluation of stability and range of motion, alongside encouraging short-term clinical observations. The reference lists three related articles; these observations should be understood in the context of the original studies.
