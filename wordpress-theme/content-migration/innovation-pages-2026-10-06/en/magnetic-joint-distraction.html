Innovation project

Magnetic joint distraction

An implant-based research concept for sustained distraction at the thumb CMC joint.

1

Rationale

Joint distraction is investigated as a way to unload an arthritic joint and potentially reduce pain. The archived project focuses on the first carpometacarpal joint.

2

Implant concept

The design proposes a pair of implants, each containing a magnet within a neutral capsule, positioned in the trapezium and first metacarpal.

3

Proposed mechanism

Like magnetic poles face one another so that a continuous repulsive force creates distraction across the joint. The concept remains a research and development pathway rather than general treatment guidance.
