Patient resources

Before-surgery preparation guide.

An organized version of the admission guidance for Dr. Ali Moradi’s hand and upper-extremity surgery service; individual surgeon and hospital instructions always take priority.

01

Implants, devices, and consultations

If a specific implant or device is required, obtain it before surgery and coordinate sterilization with the hospital department on admission. Complete every requested consultation—including anesthesia or cardiology—before admission.

02

Fasting and the day of surgery

The supplied guide states that eight hours of fasting are required and that no food or drink should be consumed from 6:00 AM on the day of surgery. Confirm the exact timing with the treating team and do not follow a conflicting instruction without approval.

03

Hygiene and preparation

Shower before admission, trim nails, and remove nail polish, jewellery, and metal objects from the hand. The guide recommends 2% chlorhexidine wash or baby shampoo and using an electric clipper—not a razor—for hair removal near the operative area.

04

Documents to bring

Bring the admission form, requested consultation reports, the required device and sterilization confirmation, radiographs, CT or MRI records, nerve-conduction studies, and summaries or reports from previous operations.

05

Cancellation

If surgery must be cancelled, notify the service by SMS at least two days in advance using +98 905 450 1141.

06

Postoperative points to understand beforehand

Do not open or wet the dressing. Follow-up is commonly one week later unless another date is given. Keep the limb in a sling and move only as permitted. Do not start antibiotics on your own. Intolerable pain or a serious problem needs prompt contact or urgent assessment.

07

Consent and acknowledgement form

Download and print the one-page form. Complete the patient and procedure fields and sign after reading it and asking your questions. This file does not replace the hospital’s official consent process.
